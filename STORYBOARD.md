# Escape from Nodnarb storyboard

Working story document for the ten-stage escape route. Each stage uses one or two still-image panels with radio copy. No full cinematics are required for the current scope.

## Direction lock

- Nodnarb is an alien wilderness. No paved roads, railings, street furniture, buildings, or civilized enemy bases.
- Routes are creature-worn passages, broken ground, switchbacks, shelves, nests, and natural choke points.
- Still panels should show the crashed ship, the squad's changing conditions, and the rescue signal getting closer.
- Final dialogue remains editable by Blake before any production voice or music work.

## Stage beats

| Stage | Frame 1 | Frame 2 | Story question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | The hull splits | The beacon answers | What woke below the wreck? |
| 02 | Ash behind us | The basin moves | Can the squad leave the crash site alive? |
| 03 | The ground breathes | Every shot calls them | Is the planet tracking heat and sound? |
| 04 | Whiteglass weather | The arch is alive | What is following the squad over the ridge? |
| 05 | A dead relay | Send the ping | Who hears the rescue signal first? |
| 06 | The planet answers | The fault opens | Why is the crystal shelf opening under them? |
| 07 | Thousands below | Run the brood channel | How large is the nest beneath the trench? |
| 08 | Night falls fast | The signal fades | Can rescue still see them in the dark? |
| 09 | Light the sky | The plain opens | What turns the whole valley toward the beacon? |
| 10 | Ninety seconds | Leave together | Can everyone reach extraction before the last carrier? |

## Panel recipe

1. One wide environmental still with the squad small in frame.
2. One close signal/threat still that leaves a question unanswered.
3. One short radio exchange, then deploy.
