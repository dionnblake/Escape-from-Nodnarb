Still, this is the one finding worth a real decision: a mobile game studio already uses "Nodnarb" as its core brand identity, in the same broad market (mobile games, same app stores). "Escape from Nodnarb" as a game title is a different string, but a player searching "Nodnarb" on Google Play or the App Store would likely see both. Recommend treating this as a genuine naming-collision risk to weigh, not a blocker to dismiss.

## Recommendation

- The full title "Escape from Nodnarb" looks clear to proceed with informally, no direct collision found anywhere it was checked.
- Before committing to it as a permanent public brand (store listing, domain purchase, social handles, any paid marketing), do a real USPTO trademark database search directly at uspto.gov and check exact domain/handle availability through an actual registrar and each platform's own signup flow. Both are quick and this research doesn't replace either.
- Worth a conscious decision, not an automatic pass: acceptable overlap with "Nodnarb Games" existing as a studio brand, or address it now (e.g., lean harder on the full "Escape from Nodnarb" title in all marketing, avoid ever shortening it to just "Nodnarb" in branding).
