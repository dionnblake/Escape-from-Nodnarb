## Full description

Your ship went down on a world with no name on any chart. Your crew calls it Nodnarb, because somebody needed to call it something.

Drag your Captain left and right while your squad auto-fires ahead of you. Choose weapon power on the left or another recruit on the right. Build a twelve-soldier firing line and push across ten hand-authored stages toward one goal: get found before Nodnarb finds you first.

Every shot you fire tells the planet where you are. That's not a mechanic, that's the whole story. Nodnarb is not a passive backdrop. It reacts to heat, to noise, to signal, and every level pushes further into ground that answers back harder.

**What you actually get:**
- One-thumb drag movement and auto-fire, playable in seconds, no tutorial wall.
- A real left-or-right choice every run: more firepower, or more crew. Never both at once.
- A ten-stage escape campaign with a real story, from a crashed ship's interior to a final extraction battle.
- Endless mode once the campaign's done, for players who just want to see how long they can hold the line.
- No forced ads. No pay-to-win. Cosmetics only, and only after you've beaten a level.

**This is not the game you think it is, and that's the point.** No gacha, no hero collection, no energy timer. Just the loop the ad promised, delivered honestly.
